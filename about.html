Our Story

Serving the Kilburn Community

Super Indian Grocery has become a familiar stop for households across Kilburn and the surrounding northern Adelaide suburbs. As a locally run grocery store, we understand what our community is looking for when it comes to cooking a favourite dish from home or simply restocking the pantry for the week ahead.

Our shelves are stocked with a broad selection of Indian groceries and cooking ingredients — from rice, flour and dal to spices, masalas, snacks, sweets, pickles and ready-to-cook items. Alongside these specialty products, we also carry everyday food essentials, so a single visit to our store can cover much of what a household needs.

Being a locally run, community-focused store matters to us. We're part of the Kilburn neighbourhood, and we aim to be a place where customers feel comfortable, welcome and able to find familiar products without having to travel far from home.

Friendly, helpful service is at the heart of how we operate. Our team is always happy to answer questions, help you locate a particular product, or point you toward something new if you're keen to try a different flavour or ingredient. We look forward to welcoming you in store.
