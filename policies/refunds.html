Refund & Returns Policy

Overview

This is a general refund and returns policy for Super Indian Grocery, an in-store Indian grocery retailer located in Kilburn, South Australia. Super Indian Grocery does not operate an online store, so all purchases and any related refund or return requests are handled in person at our physical store.

Change of Mind

Change-of-mind returns are considered at the discretion of Super Indian Grocery and may depend on the type of product purchased. Perishable items and food products are generally not eligible for a change-of-mind return once purchased, due to hygiene and food-safety reasons. If you would like to discuss a change-of-mind request, please speak with our staff in-store and we will do our best to assist you.

Faulty, Damaged or Incorrect Products

Under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL), consumers are entitled to a repair, replacement or refund where a product has a major failure, and to a repair or replacement where a product fails to be of acceptable quality. Super Indian Grocery will honour these consumer guarantees for any faulty, damaged or incorrect products purchased in-store. Please bring the product, along with your proof of purchase where possible, to the store so our staff can assess the issue and arrange an appropriate remedy.

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies the consumer guarantees, rights or remedies available to you under the Australian Consumer Law.

How to Request a Refund or Raise an Issue

The best way to raise a refund, return or product issue is to visit us in-store and speak with our staff directly. Alternatively, you can reach out via our Contact page to raise an issue. A direct phone number and email address are not yet published, so the contact form or an in-store visit are currently the best ways to get in touch with us.

This page provides general information about Super Indian Grocery's policies and does not constitute legal advice. We recommend this content be reviewed by an Australian legal professional before being relied upon as a final legal document.
