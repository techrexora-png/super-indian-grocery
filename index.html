Who We Are

Your Local Indian Grocery Store

Super Indian Grocery is a local grocery store based in Kilburn, South Australia, proudly serving the surrounding community. We stock a range of Indian groceries, cooking ingredients, snacks, spices and everyday food essentials to help you bring authentic flavours into your kitchen.

Whether you're after pantry staples for everyday cooking, a favourite snack from home, or ingredients for a special recipe, our shelves are stocked with a variety of products for households across Kilburn and nearby suburbs.

We're a locally run store that values our customers and the community we're part of, and we're always happy to help you find what you're looking for.
